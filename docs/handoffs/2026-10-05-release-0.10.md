## Decisions

- The analytics host is not in the source. The server's domain is also an owner deployment domain,
  which `tests/no-owner-data.test.ts` keeps out of everything that ships.
- The Mac App Store package carries no App Key: the sandbox cannot reach the shared file, and the
  App Privacy answers come first (B-49).
- The rest of the audit (no `send_draft`, allowance refund on 409, journal cursor, `list_rules` cap,
  HTML signature) is B-50. B-36 is narrowed to domain limits and Admin keys.
