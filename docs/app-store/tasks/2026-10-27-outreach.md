# B-181: outreach campaigns

The operator's request (2026-10-26, [the packet](2026-10-26-mail-safety-and-outreach.md) REQ-8): an agent runs a
server-side sending loop over a list of addresses and messages; researched first against the tools people use.
The research, every claim with its source: [2026-10-27-outreach-research.md](2026-10-27-outreach-research.md).

## Decisions (2026-10-27; reversible; reasons)

1. **Campaigns send only from the person's connected Gmail, Outlook and IMAP/SMTP accounts.** Cloudflare Email
   Service is transactional only ("No. Email Service supports transactional email only", research §0.1), so the
   person's Cloudflare addresses are not offered as campaign senders. The operator may reopen this when Cloudflare
   changes the rule or an address is routed through another SMTP account.
2. **Human-paced volume is the safety model, not a setting** (Gmail and Exchange both say their own accounts are not
   for bulk, research §0.2): the defaults of research §7 are the defaults here (30 a day per mailbox, ceiling 50,
   ramp-up from 10, 5 to 10 minutes between sends, Mon to Fri 09:00 to 17:00, follow-up after 3 days, stop on reply,
   company stop, bounce pause at 5%, plain text, no tracking) and the provider limits are never passed.
3. **Every campaign message can be unsubscribed from**: a signed link in the body and `List-Unsubscribe` with
   `List-Unsubscribe-Post` to the person's own server; one suppression list (addresses and domains) is checked
   before every send. Whether Gmail API and Microsoft Graph keep those headers under DKIM is measured on a real
   message before the screen promises one-click unsubscribe for those accounts.
4. **The law is the sender's**: the screen names the rules that apply (CAN-SPAM, EU consent rules that differ by
   country, CASL, research §4) and the campaign carries the sender's postal address field; Fabric Inbox does not
   decide for the person whether a recipient may be written to.

## Build, in order (each a release)

1. **Core and agent tools**: a campaign object (name, senders, window, steps up to 1 + 3 follow-ups, recipients with
   fields, `{{field}}` with fallbacks), a preview per recipient and a missing-field check, the suppression list, the
   routes and the MCP tools (create, update, add recipients, preview, launch, pause, resume, stop, report, list
   recipients, suppression), and a dry run that schedules without sending.
2. **The sending loop**: per mailbox, the rolling daily cap shared across campaigns, ramp-up, random gaps, the
   window and time zone, retry on a provider's rate error; stop on reply, pause on auto-reply, stop on bounce,
   company stop; the unsubscribe endpoint and headers; the bounce pause; the report.
3. **The screen** (scenario first, super-ux): campaigns, recipients by status, the report.
